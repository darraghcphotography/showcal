About this site

Hello! What's this? How did I get here? Who are you?

I'm Darragh - originally from Tuam, dabbled in the Déise for a few years, and now based in Dublin. You might know me from popping up at shows or rehearsals with a camera, joining the odd committee, or running around the INEC like a man possessed each June shouting at people on the stairs!

For the last couple of years, I've been maintaining a private online spreadsheet to compile shows across the country - tracking production teams, dates, and review links. As part of the AIMS National Council, I manage AIMS.ie and publish the adjudicator reviews, so the spreadsheet was my way of keeping track of them while planning which shows I could travel to see during the season. I always try to see as many as possible (whether shooting or just supporting) - plus, it makes the Awards weekend a lot more interesting!

This is not an official AIMS website. It isn't run or endorsed by the Association of Irish Musical Societies as a body - it's my own independent project, built and maintained separately from my Council role. For anything official - membership, insurance, the adjudication scheme itself, AIMS news and events - the place to go is the AIMS website and channels.

Got a burst of inspiration over the August bank holiday and decided to evolve that spreadsheet into this website using some new tools, skills, and experimentation (and to keep myself busy during the 'off' season, though I'd much rather be out snapping shows!). {{ total_societies }} societies are already on here, with awards data back to {{ historical_from }}.

How you can help

  • Got a show coming up? Make sure your society's dates are listed - log in via Society login and add them yourself.
  • Want to log society history? Drop me a message for a login code! I can give you or your local society historian access to backfill previous production history - the more data we have, the more useful this site becomes for everyone.
  • Notice dodgy data? Let me know so I can get it fixed.

All feedback is greatly appreciated. This is a voluntary passion project built for the Irish MT community, so if you find it handy, please share it around!

(And yes... I still have a couple of last season's reviews to finish publishing on the AIMS site, but I promise they'll all be up before the end of August!)
