About this site

The Unofficial AIMS Show Tracker is a personal project by Darragh C, built to give AIMS member societies, their members, and audiences a single place to browse show history, upcoming productions, and AIMS's adjudication record - {{ total_societies }} societies and awards data back to {{ historical_from }}.

This is not an official AIMS website. It isn't run or endorsed by the Association of Irish Musical Societies - it's an independent project, built and maintained separately. For anything official - membership, insurance, the adjudication scheme itself, AIMS's own news and events - the place to go is AIMS's own site and channels.

Where the data comes from

Current show listings come from AIMS member societies themselves - either imported from the show season spreadsheet, submitted by a member and checked by a moderator, or entered directly by a society's own login. The awards archive (1977-present) was originally bootstrapped from AIMS's own historical adjudication records, and is now maintained directly going forward. Synopses and rights information on individual show pages are added by a moderator, not automatically generated.
