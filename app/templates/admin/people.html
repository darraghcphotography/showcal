Same person, different spellings

The same person turns up in the archive more than once - as an award nominee, as a show credit a society typed in, with and without a fada, with and without "Fr". Recording that two spellings are one person is a note for moderators only: nothing here appears on the public site, and nothing rewrites the archive. Every award row and every credit keeps the exact text it always had, so a decision made here can be undone below.
