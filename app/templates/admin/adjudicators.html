Adjudicators

AIMS assigns one adjudicator per tier per season, not per show - recording who covered Gilbert/Sullivan each season lets you match a show's published review to its likely author, and pull up every review a given adjudicator wrote. A season/tier normally needs only the one dropdown - open "+ Add a mid-season change" for the rare case AIMS swapped adjudicators partway through, and use the note to say when/why (e.g. "took over from X in October"). Seasons with both tiers already filled in collapse out of the way below the main list.
