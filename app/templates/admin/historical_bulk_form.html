Bulk add historical productions

For backfilling a society's own "previous productions" list - no award/category data, just a record that a show happened. Paste one production per line as YEAR Title, straight off the society's own website or programme - e.g.:

1998 Ham
2005 Jekyll & Hyde (Irish Premiere)
2013 Jesus Christ Superstar (Tuam 400 Celebration Special)

A trailing (...) is saved as a note, not part of the title - so "Jekyll & Hyde (Irish Premiere)" is stored as the plain title "Jekyll & Hyde" (matching how it's spelled everywhere else on the site) with "Irish Premiere" kept as a note.

Year convention: AIMS records a show's year as one year after it actually opened (a show staged in autumn 2016 is recorded as 2017 - the same convention behind season "23/24" mapping to 2024). Leave the box below ticked if you're typing the production year as the society remembers it (the normal case); untick it only if you're entering the AIMS year directly.

Already-on-record productions (same year, title, and society) are skipped automatically, so it's safe to re-paste an extended list later without creating duplicates.
