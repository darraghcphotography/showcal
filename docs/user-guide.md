# User guide

For anyone browsing the site, or an AIMS member submitting a show.

## Browsing

The homepage lists every AIMS member society. You can narrow it down:

- **Region** - Eastern, Western, Northern, South-West, South-East, Midlands.
- **Tier** - Gilbert or Sullivan (AIMS's two competition tiers), Non-AIMS, or
  Inactive (registered but with no show on record yet - hidden by default,
  see below).
- **Society name** - a text search box.

Above the society list, **Upcoming shows** lists the next few announced
productions across every society, soonest first.

Click a society to see its full show history. Click a show to see its
production team (director, musical director, choreographer), dates, venue,
poster (if one was uploaded), a ticket link (if the society provided one),
and a link to the AIMS review once it's published.

Two other pages worth knowing about:

- **This season** - every show announced so far for the current season,
  grouped by region, next to its society. Handy for a season-at-a-glance view
  without clicking into every society individually.
- **Statistics** - the most-performed shows on the AIMS circuit (all-time and
  in the last three seasons), and the list of shows that have only ever been
  performed once.

Inactive societies (registered with AIMS but with no show on record yet)
are hidden from the browse list by default to keep it focused on active
societies. If you're a moderator and need to see them, there's a checkbox
for that (see the [moderator guide](moderator-guide.md)).

## Submitting a show

If your society has a show to add, go to **Submit a show**.

1. **Enter your invite code.** This is a short code your society secretary or
   AIMS gives out - it's what stops the form being open to random submissions
   from the public. If you don't have one, ask your society secretary, or
   contact AIMS.
2. **Fill in the form:**
   - Society and season (the season list only ever offers the current and
     next season - you can't accidentally file something under an old one).
   - Show title, opening/closing dates.
   - Whether the show is being adjudicated by AIMS (Yes/No) - most shows are;
     say No only if your society isn't entering this particular production
     for adjudication.
   - Venue, director, musical director, choreographer.
   - An optional ticket link, so visitors can click straight through to buy
     tickets.
   - An optional poster image (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF, up to 8&nbsp;MB) - it'll
     show on the show's page once approved.
3. **Submit.** That's it - a moderator reviews it before it goes live.

You won't be asked for your name or email. If you need to follow something up
after submitting, contact the moderator directly - the site doesn't collect
who submitted what beyond which invite code was used.

## What happens after you submit

Nothing appears on the public site immediately. A moderator checks the
submission first (moderation queue), and either approves it - at which point
it appears in the society's history and on the "This season" page - or
rejects it. Once the show has happened and been adjudicated, a moderator
attaches the AIMS review link, and its status changes to **Published** on the
show's page.
